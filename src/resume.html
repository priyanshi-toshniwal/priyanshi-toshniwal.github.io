Experience

Jul 2023 – Present

I joined The/Nudge in 2023 as an Associate, first working on operationalising grand challenges and designing metrics to track challenger success. As I ran everything from grand finale events to post-challenge governance, I found myself getting more drawn to research.

This is where I became a researcher. A landscape study on female labour force participation pulled me in completely, and it became the foundation for five problem statements, two of which are now live grand challenges with a combined prize purse of INR 11.75 crore.

As I became part of the Product team, I worked on the investability case for India's AgWater sector and a proposal for a multi-year, $2.2M study with the Gates Foundation on women in digital value chains. Both gave me real perspective, through primary research and direct conversations with the people closest to the work.

Now, as a Senior Associate, my work has spanned everything from leading the industry-mapped taxonomy for digital skills to authoring One Crisis Away, a public report on the climate vulnerability of 300M+ Indians under our Climate Futures theme. Most recently, I've been leading landscape research into India's MSME sector and co-facilitating ecosystem audits to shape the next phase of our work.

Jul 2022 - Mar 2023

My time at The Gender Lab taught me as much as it taught the girls I worked with. With over 300 adolescent girls, I helped them navigate the gender norms in their lives and see that their voice and choice were the most powerful tools they had in becoming leaders of their own journeys. Each session was a window into how their realities shifted across different schools and socio-economic contexts, but their real confidence showed up during the community project phase.

Watching these girls take on the issues around them, garbage management, menstrual awareness, masculinity, and set aside their fears to act on them moved me. Some of them stepped outside a two-kilometre radius of their homes for the first time to do it. Mentoring over 40 of these projects did more than create local impact; it redefined my own understanding of leadership, contextual learning, and what powerful, practical feminism looks like in action.

Oct 2020 - Apr 2022

In-mind grew out of two things: my own experience with mental health, and the years I'd spent listening to other people work through theirs. Hearing so many people taught me how much it matters to simply be heard. So when the pandemic hit and I kept thinking about children with no language for what they were feeling, navigating it alone, I wanted to build a space for exactly that.

I founded In-mind and ran it as a full-time initiative over 18 months. It started with a 3-class workshop series reaching 150+ students at Vidhyanjali International School, and what I learned there shaped everything after: a curriculum and a library of replicable session frameworks on empathy, emotional expression, and listening, built so the sessions could be reused and adapted. I designed and facilitated 15+ online workshops across age groups through 2021, alongside a steady presence through In-mind's blog and social channels that carried the conversation to 300+ young people and parents, until I paused it to go deeper into the same work through The Gender Lab fellowship.
