July 2022 – Mar 2023

The Gender Lab was an unconventional path of unlearning and self-discovery. I worked with over 300 adolescent girls, helping them navigate the gender norms in their lives and realise that their "voice and choice" were the most powerful tools in becoming leaders of their own journeys. Each session offered a window into how their realities shifted across different schools and socio-economic contexts, but their true confidence emerged during the community project phase. Watching the girls outline their neighbourhood issues and set aside their fears to take the first steps towards a solution was incredibly moving. Mentoring over 40 of these community projects did more than just create local impact; it completely redefined my own understanding of leadership, contextual learning, and what powerful, yet practical, feminism looks like in action.

Oct 2020 – Apr 2022

What began as a journey within eventually grew into "In-mind" in the world outside. Mental health has been close to my heart and mind for years, and my path started simply: by listening. Hearing hundreds of stories gave me the strength to launch In-mind—a project that was very much a reflection of my own mental health journey. When the pandemic hit and the world felt increasingly isolated, I realised how daunting it can be to navigate these challenges alone, especially when speaking up feels so difficult. I began designing and facilitating workshops that used creative methods to help people of all ages explore their emotions and well-being. This grew into a series of 15+ virtual sessions over 18 months, providing a much-needed space for connection and conversation during a time of collective loneliness.
