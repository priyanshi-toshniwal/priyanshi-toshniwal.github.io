With a strong background across social innovation, gender awareness and leadership building, empathy and mental health awareness, and corporate legal knowledge, I bring 4.5+ years of experience in the social sector through self-initiatives, fellowships, and large non-profits. My work sits at the intersection of mental health, gender, education and research, social innovation, and livelihoods. I specialise in deep research, translating insights through systems thinking, and designing and facilitating workshops rooted in empathy and meaningful understanding of people and contexts. While research holds a major space in my interests, I focus on shaping ideas that blend human-centered empathy, long-standing insight, and sharp analytical thinking. I remain committed to building work that is thoughtful, deeply grounded, and responsive to the people and systems it aims to impact. I like to explore beyond Job Descriptions, work beyond roles, and explore skill sets through learnings and trials.
