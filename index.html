I've spent 4.5+ years in the social sector, across self-initiatives, fellowships, and large non-profits. My work sits at the intersection of gender, wellbeing, education, social innovation, and livelihoods.

I work mostly in research, making sense of complex problems through systems thinking, and in designing and facilitating workshops rooted in empathy and a real understanding of people and context. What ties it together is a human-first approach: I care about staying close to the people the work is for.

I like to explore beyond job descriptions, work beyond roles, and build my skills through learning and trying things.
